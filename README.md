## The figure uses every device (2026-10-10)

## Pose-doll body (2026-10-10, "mannequin refinement" thread)
Gabriel asked for no muscular definition, like a default pose doll in a modelling program. `src/figure/dummify.py` fairs the vertex positions only (cotangent bilaplacian, strength 2000 ≈ forms under ~7 cm are smoothed away): pecs, abs, navel, kneecaps, calves and clavicles go. Hands and the egg head are pinned, soles stay on the old floor, and the legs may only lose volume (never grow), so grasps, the brace cuffs, the calf shell and THIGH_FIT still fit. Topology, skin weights and bones are unchanged.
`src/figure/rings.py` then undraws the wireframe loops that ringed the nipples and navel. The previous body is kept as `src/figure/figure_data_before_dummy.js`.
Run: `python3 dummify.py figure_data_before_dummy.js out.js 2000 40 1 1` then `python3 rings.py out.js figure_data.js 4 3 7 0`.
Toes are still separate (only lightly affected); merging them into a mitten foot was tried and left folds, so it is not in.
Doll hands (same day): `src/figure/handfair.py` (strength 0.5) smooths knuckles, tendons and nail detail into plain tube fingers with round tips, keeping finger thickness; finger bones and grasps are unchanged. It runs last, on the output of rings.py: `python3 handfair.py figure_data.js out.js 0.5 8`.
Nipples (same day): `src/figure/chestfair.py d_in d_out 1 1 0 5` (used only to trim wire stubs that dead-ended at the nipples) then `src/figure/nipfix.py` puts the nipple and navel patches on a quadratic surface fitted to the skin around them and evens out their quads.

## Site editor (2026-10-11, "editor/admin view" thread)
`src/editor.js` is an owner-only edit mode, inlined by build.sh at `<script>/*EDITOR*/</script>` right after `</main>`. In the published fork (v41+) the owner gets an **Edit** button in the top-right bar; nobody else does. It can retype any heading, paragraph, caption etc. (double-click), restyle it (font, size, weight, colour, case, align, line height, spacing, opacity), drag any text or block to move it, resize its width, set a card's side, or hide it. **Annotate** mode pins a note anywhere on the page for Claude.
- Storage: the artifact's db, declared at publish as `capabilities: {db: {rules: [{path:"", read:"view", write:"admin"}, {path:"notes", read:"admin", write:"admin"}, {path:"shots", read:"admin", write:"admin"}]}, user: {}}`. Republishing without `capabilities` keeps it. Off claude.ai (index.html, the live site) the editor appears only with `#edit` in the URL and stores in localStorage, with Export/Import JSON.
- `edits/<key>`: one doc per changed element, applied live for every viewer. Key = section `data-s` then tag+index down the DOM (`s2.div1.h21` = section 2, first div, first h2; `page.titleblock`). Fields: `fp` + `origHtml` (original text, used to find the element if keys shift; a doc whose text no longer matches is skipped, shown as stale), `html` (new text), `style` {font, size px, weight, italic, color, align, lh, ls em, tt, op}, `layout.desktop|phone` {x, y px translate, w px, side}, `hidden`. Position applies only at the width it was made (phone ≤ 720 px).
- `notes/<id>`: {n, text, status open|done, reply, s (scroll position as main.html's s), section {index, eyebrow, title}, layout, viewport, scrollY, screen {x, y, fx, fy}, target {kind text|block|3d|floor|empty, key/name/fp/rel for elements, object/point/distance/bone for the model}, camera {pos, dir, look, fov, free}}. `shots/<id>` holds a 640 px JPEG data URL of the 3D view at that moment (no HTML cards in it).
- To act on notes: `ArtifactData list notes` on the fork, reproduce a view with `__rig.setS(s)` and, if `camera.free`, `window.__cam = [pos, look]`; make the change in src, rebuild, republish, then `update` the note with `status: "done"` and a one-line `reply` (the editor shows it to Gabriel).
- To bake edits into the source: apply `html` to the element found by `origHtml`, turn `style`/`layout` into CSS in main.html, rebuild and publish, then delete the edit doc (stale docs are skipped anyway, so a baked edit never applies twice).
- `window.__rig` now also exposes `scene, renderer, controls, free` and a live `s` getter, which the editor uses for picking and snapshots. Headless checks: jsdelivr is blocked from cloud containers; route `cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/three@0.160.0/**` to a local `npm install three@0.160.0`.

## Drive games (v76–v85)
Both drive sections are games once the reader takes over (nothing shows on autopilot).
- **Scooter** (`GAME` in rigs.js; gameFrame and friends before scooterFrame): the walk crumbles away behind you, faster and faster; falling crutches, then thrown balls, then low birds; puddles (with a rubber duck) make you skid; ramps sit left or right at random with a star over each; every 5 stars boosts. Hits damage body parts, shown on the mini mannequin (`#schurt`, it gets crippled); at zero health you ragdoll. Score = metres + 10 per star. Space hops (hold for height), again in the air for a double jump; S / ▼ ducks. Mid-run the controls stay yours until you go down.
- **Scooter first-run tips** (`TUT`, `tutFrame` in rigs.js, `#sctut`): the first scooter run of a browser session teaches one move at a time, each at the moment it is needed: 1 change lanes (a crutch tips into your lane), 2 jump (two crutches across the walk) then double jump (two pairs back to back), 3 duck (a low bird that follows your lane). As each hazard reaches the point where the move works, the world slows to a crawl and the tip shows (finger animation on phones, keys on keyboards); making the move brings the speed back. While tips run, hits cost no health, the walk doesn't crumble and random hazards wait; a missed move is taught again (three misses and it moves on). Progress is in sessionStorage `gp.scooter.tut`, so a new session teaches again; Skip tips ends them. Keyboard riders also get Hold W if they stall, and the steer tip if stuck on the curb.
- **Couch**: big cues (`couchCues`): 3, 2, 1, GO, then DRIFT and Keep drifting on the roundabout, Return to start after the lap, the lap time at gate 1. `#cohud` lap clock (the score is the full-course time, lower wins) and the drift bar: sliding fills it, Space or a double tap/click fires a boost (`NITRO`, `couchBoost`; Space was the handbrake). `#codial` speed dial, 0 to 40 km/h. Gate chevrons turn round on the way home.
- **Leaderboards** (`LB` in rigs.js, `#lboard`): a live board per game, middle left: small (top 3) while you play, full with a name box (up to 10 characters) when you go down or finish a lap. On claude.ai they live in the artifact db (`lb/<user id>` {tag, sc, lap}; rules: lb read view / write owner, lb/{self} write interact, so view-only link visitors can read but not post). On gabrielprice.com the page uses the Worker's `/api/lb` (repo worker/lb.js).

## Section ruler (2026-10-13, "Scroll progress and section directory" thread)
`#secnav` in main.html is a hairline ruler in the right gutter with one tick per `section[data-s]`. A longer tick marks each build's first section, which is the one carrying `data-ch` (the chapter name). `data-nav` overrides a row's label, otherwise the row uses the section's heading. The ruler fills violet with the scroll and flashes a build's name as you enter it. Hover (or tap, on touch) unfolds the labelled index, and a row scrolls to its section: the key pose on desktop, the docked card on phones. Its own small script computes `s` exactly like `readScroll()`, so it runs without WebGL. It hides under `body.playing`. A new section gets a tick automatically, and a new build needs `data-ch` on its first section.
