Mannequin lineup

Nine body styles for the site figure, each built on the same skeleton as the current one. Arms are lowered here for display only. Drag a figure to turn it, and reply in the thread with the number you want.

01–04

Faceted

Flat-shaded low-poly, like the reference you sent. The same body cut to different triangle counts, in two builds: the soft doll and a chiseled V-taper.

01Chiseled

Broad chest block, shoulder caps, narrow waist, calf and forearm swell. Closest to your reference.

02Faceted doll

The softer doll proportions, with straight sides and a rounder chest, cut into planes.

03Crystal

The chiseled body cut down hard. Big planes, very graphic, reads like a game proxy.

04Fine facet

The chiseled body with small facets. Smooth from a distance, faceted in close-ups.

05–09

Other directions

Smooth-shaded technical styles from the first round of ideas, kept for comparison.

05Vinyl doll

Blended capsules and an egg head, like a sculpting app's default base mesh.

06Drawing manikin

Separate pieces on ball joints, like an artist's wooden manikin. Segmented, like the Xbot you passed on.

07Crash-test dummy

Seam rings at every joint, a waist and neck break, and quadrant calibration targets.

08Box proxy

Rounded boxes for every segment, like a blocking-out rig before modelling.

09Mocap suit

Smooth body with suit seams and tracking markers on the joints and spine.

Heights are to scale: every figure is 173.8 cm on a 66 cm plinth, the same as the site figure. Whichever style you pick gets skinned to the site's skeleton with the quad wireframe, and its hands get finger joints so they still grip the crutch, scooter bars and wheel.
